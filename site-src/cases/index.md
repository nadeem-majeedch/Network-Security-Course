# Case Studies

One hundred progressive problem-solving cases run through the course —
twenty beginner, twenty-five intermediate, thirty advanced, twenty-five
expert — distributed across the eight modules and anchored to specific
lectures.

> **All scenarios are simulated fiction.** Organizations, hosts, telemetry,
> and people are invented for teaching. Nothing in this collection
> represents a real incident or real entity.

## The classroom protocol

Every case follows the same five-step rhythm:

1. **Case on the projector** — scenario, evidence, task visible to all.
2. **~5 minutes reasoning** — individually or in small groups.
3. **Approach discussion** — plausible solutions compared before any reveal.
4. **Model solution reveal** — instructor walks the reasoning, not just the answer.
5. **Tradeoffs & common mistakes** — where the defensible alternatives diverge.

## Tier structure

| Tier | Cases | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner | cs-001–020 | Identify, classify, explain a single mechanism |
| Intermediate | cs-021–045 | Design or diagnose with stated tradeoffs |
| Advanced | cs-046–075 | Multi-constraint judgment, quantitative prioritization |
| Expert | cs-076–100 | Program-level decisions under uncertainty; the Meridian capstone arc |
