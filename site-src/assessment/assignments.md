# Assignments

Seven individual assignments (35% of the final grade, 5% each) — each one
is a design-and-justify task, not a report. You produce an artifact and
defend the choices in it.

| # | Week | Assignment | Artifact you produce | CLOs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | [Segmentation design](assignments/assignment-01-week06-segmentation-design.md) | Zone model + ACL/egress policy for a two-site company | CLO-3, CLO-4 |
| 2 | 6 | [TLS deployment audit](assignments/assignment-02-week07-tls-deployment-audit.md) | Audit report on lab endpoints' TLS configuration | CLO-5, CLO-6 |
| 3 | 8 | [VPN access design](assignments/assignment-03-week09-vpn-access-design.md) | Remote-access architecture: full vs split tunnel, IPsec vs WireGuard-class | CLO-6 |

| 6 | 13 | [IR runbook development](assignments/assignment-06-week13-ir-runbook-development.md) | Scenario runbook + tabletop plan with injects | CLO-11, CLO-12 |

## Expectations

- **Design rationale over volume.** A defensible one-page design beats a
  ten-page dump. Every constraint you cite must be one from the brief or
  the course, not invented.
- **Evidence, not assertion.** Where the brief provides telemetry or lab
  output, cite line numbers/timestamps in your reasoning.
- **Individual work.** Discussion of concepts is fine; artifacts must be
  your own. Cite tools and any AI assistance in the submission header.
- **Late policy:** 10%/day to 5 days (see [syllabus](../course/syllabus.md)).

Briefs are distributed through the LMS on their release week; the links
above are the in-repository student briefs. Grading keys and band
anchors are instructor-only.
