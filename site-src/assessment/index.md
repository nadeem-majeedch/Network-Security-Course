# Assessment Information

Grades come from six components, weighted per the syllabus. Every graded
item maps to specific CLOs; the full blueprint lives in the syllabus and the
instructor's assessment coverage report (not published here).

| Component | Weight | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Graded quizzes (×5) | 15% | [Quizzes & Self-Checks](quizzes.md) |
| Lab practicals | 20% | Four graded labs (06, 07, 10, 11 — Pract-1…4) — graded via [Lab Workbook](../labs/index.md) rubrics |
| Assignments (×7) | 35% | [Assignments](assignments.md) |
| Midterm (Week 8) | 10% | [Examinations](exams.md) |
| Capstone + defense | 15% | [Capstone Project](capstone.md) |
| Final (Week 16) | 5% | [Examinations](exams.md) |

## Weekly rhythm

- **Self-check quizzes** (ungraded) exist for every week without a graded
  quiz — they preview the next graded item's question style.
- **Assignments** release on their syllabus week (see the
  [calendar](../course/calendar.md)) and are individual work unless stated.
- **Case sets A/B/C** are the graded case-study rotations; the evaluation
  rubric rewards reasoning quality, not answer match.

## Integrity & AI-use rules

- Cite tools, datasets, and any AI assistance in your submission header.
- Lab evidence must come from your own lab-range work — fabricated captures fail.
- Quizzes/exams are closed-book; the capstone defense is open-notes on your own artifacts.

## Where to find things

- **Quiz/exam/assignment briefs:** distributed in class and via the LMS;
  question-level material is not published on this site.
- **Past-paper style practice:** the weekly self-check quizzes and the
  question bank's *practice set* (distributed by the instructor).
- **Marking queries:** within one week of marks posting, per university policy.
