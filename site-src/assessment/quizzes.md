(15 marks, 20 minutes, in-class) and **eleven ungraded self-checks** that
preview the next graded item's question style.

## Question style (all units)

- MCQs with one defensible answer — distractors are the documented
  misconceptions, not random wrong statements.
- Short answers tagged with Bloom's level (remember → evaluate).
- At least one **interpretation item**: a diagram, a capture excerpt, an
  ACL, or a log line you reason from directly.

## What is and isn't public

The questions you'll be graded on are distributed through the LMS at
quiz time. The weekly **self-check** questions are distributed in class
the same week. Model answers and grading notes are instructor-only and
never published on this site. Practice material = the self-checks +
the [case studies](../cases/index.md) — the case protocol is the best
exam rehearsal there is.
