# Syllabus

**Network Security** — BS Computer Science / BS Data Science, 7th semester.
16 weeks · 32 lectures × 2 h (64 contact hours) · 16 embedded labs ·
100 case studies · capstone with oral defense.

## Course description

Networks fail open, and attackers read protocols more carefully than their
designers did. This course teaches you to read networks the way both
defenders and attackers do: protocol fluency first (Ethernet through TLS),
then the abuse mechanics that exploit protocol shortcuts, then the
architecture, cryptography, detection, and response disciplines that contain
them. Everything practical happens in an isolated lab range, on prepared
evidence, or in an instructor-provisioned cloud sandbox. Full text:
[Course Description](description.md).

## Prerequisites

- Computer Networks (layer models, TCP/IP addressing, routing basics)
- Operating systems (processes, permissions, Linux command line)
- One programming course (Python preferred for analysis scripting)
- Data Science students: the networks-track cases map your pipeline and
  cluster environments directly; no additional networking background assumed
  beyond the above.

## Policies

- **Attendance:** labs are graded on submitted evidence, not seat time; but
  case-study sessions and the IR simulation cannot be made up individually.
- **Late work:** 10%/day to a maximum of 5 days, then by instructor
  discretion; the capstone draft has no late window (feeds week-16 defense).
- **Collaboration:** labs and cases are collaborative; assignments and
  quizzes are individual; the capstone is team-reported but individually
  defended.
- **Ethics:** all techniques are practiced only inside the authorized lab
  range. Unauthorized scanning or testing of any system — including campus
  networks — is a disciplinary matter, full stop.
