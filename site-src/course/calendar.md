# 16-Week Teaching Calendar

> **One-click version:** the [Semester Calendar](calendar-generated.md) is
> generated from the finalized course content and can show real dates for
> any semester start — configure `docs-meta/calendar-config.json` and
> regenerate. A printable PDF-ready HTML and an .ics calendar file are
> produced alongside it (see `docs-meta/calendar-instructions.md`).

Two lectures per week (2 hours each), one lab per week, quizzes at
weeks 2/4/5/10/14, graded case sets in weeks 4–5/8/13, midterm in week
8, capstone defense in week 16.

## Notes

- **Case sets** run in weeks 4–5 (Set A), week 8 (Set B, with the
  midterm), and week 13 (Set C) — see
  [assessment information](../assessment/index.md).
- **Graded lab practicals** land in weeks 6, 7, 10, 11 (Pract-1…4).
- The midterm covers weeks 1–8 (Modules 1–4); the final covers weeks
  9–16 with an embedded forensic practical on course datasets.
- Detailed lecture pages: [Lecture index](../lectures/index.md).
