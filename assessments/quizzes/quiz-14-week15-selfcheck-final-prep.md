# Self-Check Quiz 14 — Week 15 (Final Preparation, L17–L32)

> Not graded — a final-exam warm-up spanning weeks 9–15. Answers in the
> course answer key.

1. **(MCQ)** Cloud egress analysis differs from on-prem primarily because:
   A. Payloads are visible by default · B. Flow logs + identity/API logs
   are first-class, payload capture is not · C. NAT hides everything ·
   D. No logs exist

2. **(MCQ)** A vulnerability with CVSS 9.8 that is *not reachable* from
   any network path should be prioritized by:
   A. Its base score alone · B. Environmental adjustments (reachability,
   exposure, compensating controls) · C. Publication date · D. Vendor
   marketing

3. **(MCQ)** The correct first IR action upon an IDS high-severity alert:
   A. Contain the host immediately · B. Corroborate across a second
   source, then contain per reversibility · C. Email the CSO · D. Reimage

4. **(MCQ)** Chain of custody is best served by:
   A. Screenshots · B. Documented acquisition, hashing, storage, and
   access records · C. Copies on USB sticks · D. Verbal handover

5. **(MCQ)** Zero-trust phase gates (as designed in cs-093) exist to:
   A. Slow procurement · B. Convert each tranche into measured evidence
   before the next · C. Avoid board reporting · D. Replace pilots

6. **(Short)** Why is "MTTD improved from 40 days to 9 days" a stronger
   board metric than "we installed 40 detection rules"? *(2 marks)*

7. **(Diagram)** A diagram shows a hybrid estate: on-prem DCs ↔ site-to-site
   VPN ↔ cloud VPC (web/app/DB) with VPC flow logs on. Mark the two
   telemetry sources that see a *cloud-initiated* attack first, and the
   one that never sees it. *(2 marks)*

8. **(Short)** Connect one *detection* concept (module 6) to one *response*
   decision (module 7) in ≤3 sentences. *(2 marks)*
