   A. Rebuild everything immediately · B. Reversible actions first,
   irreversible when scoped · C. Wait for certainty · D. Power off all
   servers

   A. Analyst overtime · B. Attacker presence from initial access to

   incidents, and why. *(2 marks)*

   the two checks before acting on containment. *(2 marks)*
