# Self-Check Quiz 16 — Week 16 (Capstone Defense Preparation)

> Not graded — rehearsal for the oral defense (rubric:
> `assessments/capstone/defense-guide.md`). Answers in the course answer key.

1. **(MCQ)** In a defense, the strongest answer to "what did you cut and
   why?" is:
   A. "We didn't cut anything" · B. A named deferral with the accepted
   risk, the pairing control, and who it was agreed with · C. "Time ran
   out" · D. "That was out of scope"

2. **(MCQ)** A panel asks how your design "fails silently." The best
   response names:
   A. Nothing — designs don't fail silently · B. A specific drift/decay
   mode plus its review cadence and owner · C. The vendor's SLA ·
   D. The budget

3. **(MCQ)** Your capstone claims "MTTD would improve." The panel's best
   follow-up is:
   A. "Which vendor?" · B. "What measured evidence or pilot result backs
   that number?" · C. "How much does it cost?" · D. "Who built it?"

4. **(MCQ)** In the walkthrough of your incident-handling exercise, each
   decision should cite:
   A. The team's mood · B. Its evidence basis and reversibility class ·
   C. The tool's brand · D. The deadline only

5. **(MCQ)** The final board paragraph of your report should contain:
   A. Everything you did · B. Incident/response summary, honest limits,
   and a specific ask · C. Only good news · D. Raw logs

6. **(Short)** State the one question your team *cannot* currently answer
   about your own design, and how you would close it. *(2 marks)*

7. **(Diagram)** Your capstone architecture diagram is challenged: "which
   single component, if down, breaks the design?" Mark the component on
   your diagram and state the fallback you designed (or should have). *(2 marks)*

8. **(Short)** Give the one-sentence version of your capstone's main risk
   reduction, phrased for a non-technical board member. *(2 marks)*

**Total: 10 marks**
