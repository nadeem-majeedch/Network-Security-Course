# Answer Key — Lab-16 (Capstone IR Simulation)

## Staging ground truth (bundle design)
Bundle = fresh staged corpus (same generator family, new seed) + log extracts
+ the **re-compromise tell**: one "restored" host resumes beaconing *after*
its declared-clean timestamp — visible only if the recovery go/no-go gate
actually re-examines post-restoration telemetry. Teams that gate-check catch
it; teams that rubber-stamp ship it. (Instructor: seed/inject IDs are
recorded on the session config sheet — regenerate via the pinned generator
command in the session runbook.)

- Ladder-ordered containment with approvers named per rung.

  telemetry review is the gate that does it).

## Q&A bank (draws from manual misconception bank §3 — pick 4–6 per team)
1. "Your containment blocked X — what did that cost you in visibility?"
2. "You scored severity P1 on what evidence exactly?" (rubric audit)
3. "Is the encryption here protecting confidentiality, authentication, or
   integrity — and how do you know?" (E-A-I precision)
4. "What did your re-scan verify that your change log claimed?" (verify stage)
5. "The evidence doesn't show attribution — what can you say, honestly?"
6. "Which single control, present earlier, collapses most of this case — and
   what did it cost?" (costed judgment, not slogan)
7. "Where does this tunnel/trust boundary end?" (module synthesis probe)
