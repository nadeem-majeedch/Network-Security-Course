# Capstone Rubric — Instructor Band Anchors

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY.** Per-criterion anchors for consistent marking
> across panels. Spot-check rule: verify one "tested" claim per team by
> re-running its evidence (5 min) — fabrication caps at 54 per the
> rubric's hard rules.

## 1. Architecture & design quality (25)

- **Outstanding (21–25):** zones justified by sensitivity+behavior with a
  flow matrix consistent with the diagram; every control carries a
  verification method *and* its cost; deferral states risk + pairing +
  who agreed. The org profile's specific pains (hospital = IoT safety,
  fintech = cardholder scope, university = 2,000 BYOD, logistics = EDI)
  visibly shaped the design.
- **Proficient (15–20):** sensible zones; some controls unverified;
  deferral present but risk vague.
- **Developing (8–14):** textbook zones pasted; no deferral; budget
  ignored.
- **Insufficient (<8):** flat network with a firewall named "zero trust."

## 2. Threat model (15)

- **Outstanding (12–15):** threats trace to the profile (the hospital's
  vendor remote path, the fintech's payment segment); each threat names
  the *chain* its control breaks (entry → lateral → exfil), not a
  product.
- **Proficient (8–11):** STRIDE-ish list with plausible mappings.
- **Developing (<8):** generic "malware, hackers, insider" list.

## 3. Build evidence (20)

- **Outstanding (16–20):** ≥4 controls with configs + before/after
  replay on course datasets; detection rule shown with FP profile;
  tested-vs-assumed honest per item; a peer could re-run the evidence.
- **Proficient (10–15):** configs present; replay thin; labeling partial.
- **Developing (<10):** screenshots without reproduction path; "tested"
  claims without artifacts.

## 4. Exercise learning (15)

- **Outstanding (12–15):** decisions with reversibility labels; the
  misses are *the design's own* (not the injector's); amendments owned
  and dated; at least one finding traceable to a D1 choice.
- **Proficient (8–11):** record complete; lessons generic.
- **Developing (<8):** "we contained everything" narrative.

## 5. Report quality (15)

- **Outstanding (12–15):** board paragraph in cs-094 structure (≤120
  words, limits + ask); honest-limits section names untested ground;
  prose carries sources/confidence per the course register.
- **Proficient (8–11):** complete but limit-light.
- **Developing (<8):** marketing prose; no limits section.

## 6. Defense (10)

- **Outstanding (8–10):** weakness offered unprompted with fallback;
  answers cite evidence chains; survives an adversarial chain ("fails
  silently → who watches the watcher?") by naming reviewer cadence.
- **Proficient (5–7):** composed; evidence-cited; concessions improvised.
- **Developing (<5):** deflects; unfamiliar with own build.

## Panel calibration

- Panels of 3 (architect, SOC lead, DS faculty archetypes) score
  independently on criterion 6 before conferring; delta >3 points
  triggers a 5-minute reconciliation.
